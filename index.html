Model 2: Parametric Landed Cost Iceberg Model

Purchasing managers often fall into the trap of lowest-unit cost. This parametric model simulates the impact of hidden supply risks (scrap, freight spikes, tariffs, safety carrying inventory) based on live variables.

Variable Model Inputs

Base Unit Purchase Cost ($) $10.00

The raw invoice price charged directly by the best cost country supplier.

Freight & Customs Routing Index (%) 45%

Includes international ocean/air freight, drayage, port congestion surcharges, and local duties.

Defect Escape & Quality Cost Index (%) 35%

Captures sorting, regional assembly rework, scrap rates, and localized customer failures.

Tariffs & Compliance Buffers (%) 40%

Compensates for shifting political tariffs, carbon border adjustments, and compliance filing fees.

Safety Buffer Inventory Carrying Cost (%) 25%

Storage, financing, and obsolescence costs of maintaining safety buffer stocks due to delivery delays.
